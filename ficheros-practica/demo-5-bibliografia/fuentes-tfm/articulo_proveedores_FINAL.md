Supplier selection criteria for compostable packaging in European B2B food markets

Katrin Müller y Sophie Laurent

Documento ficticio para practicar: la revista, los autores y los datos no existen.

Abstract

This article analyses how food manufacturers and food-service chains in France and Germany select suppliers of compostable packaging. Based on a survey of 186 purchasing managers, we find that certification is treated as a market entry requirement, while delivery reliability and price differentiate between certified suppliers. Food manufacturers place more weight on technical documentation; food-service chains on volume capacity.

Key findings

1. Certification (EN 13432) is required by 91% of respondents but explains little of the final choice.
2. Delivery reliability is the strongest predictor of supplier retention.
3. Small suppliers are perceived as more flexible but less reliable.
